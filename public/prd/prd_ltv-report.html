⚐ v2 — Revisions after dbt verification
The v1 PRD assumed cohorts were defined by "first program registration" with revenue pulled from a UNION of three registration tables. A scan of data_warehouse showed two issues: (1) cohort entry should be any first purchase — rental, program, league, or membership — not just programs; and (2) the actions mart is already a unified purchase ledger across all product types, with a product_type discriminator and proper recognized-revenue columns. The UNION approach is unnecessary; actions is the source. The revenue column is resource_non_tax_recognition_delta (GAAP recognized revenue), not amount_paid filtered to action_name = 'payment'.

1
Purpose
▾

This report enables facility managers at Bond Sports partner organizations to understand how much long-term revenue a customer generates based on the first thing they ever bought — whether that was an ice rental, a Learn to Skate enrollment, a league signup, or a membership. By surfacing that a Learn to Skate enrollee produces $1,284 in average revenue over three years versus $391 for a rental-only walk-in, facilities can make smarter decisions about where to invest marketing dollars and which entry paths to prioritize.

For Bond, this report is a CS tool: it can be shown during account reviews to demonstrate the ROI of specific entry paths and drive upsell conversations around high-value categories. It is also a direct input into the broader LTV narrative Bond needs to tell investors and operators.

Layout & Tab Structure
▾
Tab 1 — LTV Overview

KPI tiles → filter bar → bar chart (avg 36-mo LTV by first-purchase category, color-coded above/at/below average) → cohort comparison heatmap table (LTV at 6, 12, 24, 36 months per category) → top customers by lifetime revenue. Categories: Rental, Program, League, Membership. Within Program and League, an optional secondary breakdown by program_code (e.g. "LTS Level 1", "Adult Hockey") for facilities that want finer detail.

Tab 2 — Purchase Pathing

Two stacked Sankey charts. The summary Sankey shows the one-step flow across all entry categories simultaneously (first purchase → second purchase), good for a portfolio view. The deep-dive Sankey lets you select a single entry category and traces the full multi-step path (up to 4 levels for high-volume categories) with hover tooltips showing counts and percentages at each step. A "Top Pathways" table below surfaces the highest-volume and highest-LTV two-step combinations.

Tab 3 — Cohort Timeline

D3 line chart: X-axis = months 0–36 since first purchase, Y-axis = cumulative avg recognized revenue. One line per entry category, labeled at the right edge. Reference line at overall avg LTV. Supporting table shows key inflection points and the month-range where each category's revenue accelerates.

Tab 4 — Customer Explorer

Live-searchable table of individual customers. Columns: name, organization, entry category, full purchase journey (shown as chips with category icons), total purchases, total spend, years active, status. Filterable by entry category and status. Intended for CS account review prep and spot-checking model accuracy.

Data Requirements
▾

All fields below resolve directly from the existing actions mart (models/marts/actions.sql). No UNION across registration tables is required — actions is already the unified ledger across product types.

FieldSourceTransformationNotes
participant_user_id actions.participant_user_id None Primary customer key throughout. Already populated on actions for all purchase types.
organization_id actions.organization_id None Cohort scope. A user playing at two facilities is two separate customers.
product_type actions.product_type Map to friendly entry-category label Discriminator values verified in i_mapped_line_items.sql: 'reservation' → Rental, 'registration' → Program, 'league_registration' → League, 'membership' → Membership. Filter out: 'goods', 'convenience_fee', 'tax'.
first_purchase_at Derived MIN(action_time) per (participant_user_id, organization_id) where action_name = 'line_item_create' and product_type in entry scope Use the line-item-create event time, not first payment — handles rentals (no registered_at) uniformly with programs.
entry_category Derived product_type of the row where rank = 1 (mapped to friendly label) The cohort assignment key.
entry_program_code actions.program_code (Program/League rows) — fallback actions.product_code From the rank-1 row Secondary cohort label for Program and League categories. Stable across seasons. Null for Rental and Membership.
months_since_first_purchase Derived DATEDIFF(month, first_purchase_at, action_time) Bucket 0–36 for time-windowed LTV calculations
recognized_revenue actions.resource_non_tax_recognition_delta SUM per user, windowed by months_since_first GAAP recognized revenue. Already net of refunds/voids/discounts (deltas can be negative). Do not filter on action_name — recognition spans many action types (event_occur, slot_occur, punch_pass_redemption, membership_elapse, etc.).
Known Data Quality Issues
IssueImpactMitigation
No sequential ordering in source Cannot simply read "purchase #1, #2, #3" — must derive Rank line-item-create actions per (participant_user_id, organization_id) by action_time ASC using ROW_NUMBER()
Cross-org users A participant_user_id at two facilities could contaminate cohorts Scope all cohort logic to (participant_user_id, organization_id) pairs
Same-timestamp ties Two purchases in the same transaction tie on action_time Add line_item_sequence as a tiebreaker in the ORDER BY clause
Refunds/voids as negative deltas A full refund of a first purchase could leave a user with net-zero or negative LTV Acceptable — that's the GAAP truth. Flag users with all-negative LTV for QA review only.
Anonymous / non-attributed rows Some actions may have NULL participant_user_id (e.g. some org-level rentals) Filter WHERE participant_user_id IS NOT NULL in the ranking model. Quantify the excluded rows in QA.

Recommended dbt Model Architecture
▾

Two new models (down from three in v1 — the UNION layer is no longer needed). Both materialize as table; ranking and windowing are too expensive to recompute on every query.

int_customer_purchases_ordered
Intermediate model. Ranks each user's purchases chronologically per organization, from the actions mart.
Source: {{ ref('actions') }} filtered to action_name = 'line_item_create' and product_type IN ('reservation', 'registration', 'league_registration', 'membership')
ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY participant_user_id, organization_id ORDER BY action_time ASC, line_item_sequence ASC) AS purchase_rank
Capture entry_category, entry_program_code, first_purchase_at from rank-1 row (window broadcast)
Output: one row per purchase per user per org, with rank and entry-cohort metadata
↓
mart_customer_ltv
Final mart. Joins the ranked purchases back to actions for full recognized-revenue accumulation, then pre-aggregates LTV by entry category.
Join int_customer_purchases_ordered (rank = 1 metadata) to actions on (participant_user_id, organization_id)
Compute months_since_first = DATEDIFF(month, first_purchase_at, action_time)
SUM resource_non_tax_recognition_delta per user per month bucket; cumulative window
Aggregate to AVG LTV at 6, 12, 24, 36 months per (entry_category, entry_program_code, organization_id)
Output: user-grain rows for the Customer Explorer tab; cohort aggregates for the other three tabs

9
Out of Scope
▾
  • ✕Multi-touch attribution — only the first purchase drives cohort assignment
  • ✕Predictive or projected LTV — realized historical recognized revenue only
  • ✕Sub-daily refresh
  • ✕Cross-org customer tracking — a user appearing at two facilities is treated as two separate customers
  • ✕Individual PII export from the report itself
  • ✕Revenue attribution to marketing channels (source of acquisition) — separate future workstream
  • ✕Retail / goods purchases as entry points — a $5 water bottle does not define a customer's journey. Goods revenue earned after the cohort entry purchase still accrues to LTV; goods just cannot be the entry trigger.
  • ✕Convenience fees — excluded from both entry definition and LTV totals

Open Questions
▾
  • ⚠
    Q1 — Punch pass purchases as a fifth entry category Packages (punch passes) are product_type = 'package' in actions. They were not in the four categories confirmed for v2 (Rental / Program / League / Membership). A parent buying a 10-class punch pass before enrolling is a meaningful "first purchase" — but it could also be argued punch passes are just a payment method for programs. Decision needed: treat as a fifth entry category, fold into Program, or exclude entirely.
  • ⚠
    Q2 — Rental entry label granularity For the Rental category, should the cohort sub-label be the slot's facility / activity (e.g. "Ice Rink Rental", "Studio Rental") or just a flat "Rental" bucket? actions.slot_name, activity_code, and facility_code are all available. Recommend activity_code as default but needs confirmation from a facility manager about what they actually slice on.
  • ⚠
    Q3 — Minimum purchases threshold Should customers with only 1 lifetime purchase be included in LTV averages? Including them pulls averages down. Recommend excluding (≥2 purchases) as default, with a filter toggle.
  • ⚠
    Q4 — Treatment of family / multi-participant invoices A parent invoice with multiple participant_user_ids (kids on different programs) creates one purchase row per participant in actions. Confirm this is the desired behavior — each kid gets their own LTV journey scoped to the parent's organization — rather than rolling up to the paying user.

Success Criteria
▾
  • ✓
    LTV figures are reproducible: manually summing resource_non_tax_recognition_delta for a known org's top 10 customers and dividing by user count matches the report within rounding tolerance.
  • ✓
    "First purchase" is correctly identified: for 5 manually verified users — including at least one rental-first and one membership-first — the model assigns the category matching their chronologically earliest line-item-create action.
  • ✓
    Cohort table ranks correctly: entry categories are sorted in descending 36-month LTV order and match a manual spreadsheet calculation for a single org.
  • ✓
    Recognized revenue ties to finance: total recognized revenue across all cohorts in a window equals the same SUM of resource_non_tax_recognition_delta from the existing Revenue Recognition report for the same window (excluding goods/convenience_fee/tax). Off-by-cohort-filter is the only acceptable variance.
  • ✓
    Performance: report loads in under 10 seconds for a single-org filter; under 30 seconds for all-orgs.
  • ✓
    Pathing Sankey is coherent: the sum of all flows out of each entry-category node equals the total customer count for that category in the cohort table.
