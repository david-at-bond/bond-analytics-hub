01
Purpose

The Utilization Report gives facility operators a clear picture of how their space is performing — what's booked, what's empty, and what it's costing them. It enables operators to answer: "When are my prime hours being wasted, and how much money am I leaving on the table?"

Internally, Bond uses it to demonstrate platform value, support upsell conversations, and surface underperforming facilities before they churn. This report is a level-up from the prior Qlik version, adding forward-looking projections, prime/non-prime segmentation, and an interactive prime hours definition layer.

02
Audience & Use Cases
  • Facility operators — primary users. Weekly or monthly review of their own facility's performance. Looking for patterns (dead Tuesday afternoons, overbooked Friday evenings) and revenue opportunities.
  • Bond CS team — uses during customer success reviews to show operators their utilization trends and prompt conversations about schedule optimization.
  • Bond internal ops/exec — periodic benchmarking across facilities. Not the primary audience; the detail table supports export for this use case.

04
Layout & Structure

Tab structure:

  1. Utilization Summary ← primary tab; operators live here
  2. Revenue Analysis ← revenue per hour, opportunity cost drill-down
  3. Raw Data ← 5-minute model; for operators who want slot-level export

Utilization Summary tab layout:

[Section header: 📊 + title + subtitle] [KPI tiles row: Util% | Rev/Hr | Opp Cost | Booked Hrs | Overbooking Events] [Filter bar: Date Range | Org | Facility | Activity/Sport | Day Type | Weekday/Weekend | Prime/Non-Prime | Reset] [Prime Hours Definition — Sigma Input Table (collapsible): facility → prime start → prime end] ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── [Heatmap: Day of Week × Hour of Day — color = utilization %, tooltip = revenue] [Bar chart: Booked vs Available hours by day (stacked, future dates projected)] ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── [Detail table: one row per facility/org] Columns: Org | Facility | Available Hrs | Booked Hrs | Util% | Prime Util% | Non-Prime Util% | Revenue | Rev/Hr | Opp Cost

Revenue Analysis tab layout:

[KPI tiles: Total Revenue | Rev/Hr | RevPAH | YoY change] [Line chart: Daily revenue actual vs projected] [Bar chart: Revenue by activity type] [Table: Revenue breakdown by hour bucket]

📐
Opportunity cost formula:
Opportunity Cost = SUM(CASE WHEN is_booked = 0 AND is_available = 1 THEN avg_rate_per_hour ELSE 0 END)
⚠️
Overutilization: Report rows where booking_count > 1 as a separate KPI and flag in the detail table. Do NOT deduplicate — show true double-booking count.
⚠️
5-minute model (fct_utilization_5m): Feed the Raw Data tab only. Same schema as hourly but at 5m grain. Heavy — do not use for summary calculations. Consider a row limit or pre-aggregated materialization for Sigma query performance.
07
Refresh & Performance
  • Cadence: Daily (overnight dbt run); data as of previous day EOD
  • Latency: ≤24 hours; no real-time requirement

Performance concerns:

  • Hourly model: should be fine for summary queries with proper partitioning by date
  • 5-minute model: high row count — Raw Data tab must paginate or limit to a short date range by default (suggest max 7-day window on load)
  • Prime hours Input Table join adds a cross-join per facility — keep facility count reasonable; index on facility_id
08
Out of Scope
✕ Real-time or sub-hourly utilization for the main report (that's the 5m raw tab only)
✕ Pricing optimization recommendations (out of scope for v1)
✕ Cross-facility benchmarking rankings visible to operators (internal use only)
✕ Integration with external booking platforms not in Bond's system
✕ Staff scheduling or labor cost analysis

Success Criteria
Utilization % matches manual calculation from a known test facility for a known date range
Prime vs. non-prime split is configurable per facility via Input Table and changes KPIs correctly
Future dates show projected revenue from confirmed bookings; historical shows actuals
Opportunity cost figure is validated against a known empty-slot scenario
Overutilization events count matches a manual audit of double-booked slots
5m raw data tab loads a 7-day window in <10 seconds
Operators can filter to their org without seeing other orgs' data (pending RLS decision)
