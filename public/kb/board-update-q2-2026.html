What We've Built

📊
Financial Reporting Suite
Customer-Facing Product · Sigma
35 production reports across 6 Sigma workbooks, used by enterprise sports facilities to close their monthly books. Built on top of Bond's Snowflake warehouse — replacing a legacy Qlik system that couldn't handle accrual accounting at scale. Enterprise customers use these reports to pass annual audits, reconcile Stripe deposits, and file GL entries.
AR Aging — accounts receivable by age, FIFO payment application, exportable to GL systems
Revenue Recognition — recognized vs. deferred at the GL level; deferred balance schedule for audit footnotes
All Financial Activity — the unified financial ledger replacing Qlik's all-in-one export
Payments & Cash Collection — Stripe reconciliation, ACH tracking, deposit verification
Trending Analysis — GL-level year-over-year comparison, launched Q1 2026
Public Skate, Utilization, Churn/Membership — operational reporting now in active development
🗺️
R&A Roadmap Dashboard
Internal Leadership Tool · React
A real-time visibility layer built for non-technical leadership. Pulls live from JIRA, translates engineering tickets into plain English using AI, and surfaces customer impact — which accounts are affected by active work, who on the team is carrying load, and what's coming next quarter.
AI-powered summaries: JIRA ticket language rewritten for C-suite reading, automatically
Customer tagging: see which enterprise accounts are affected by active tickets
Team load view: active work per engineer, visible in one screen
JIRA write-back: update priorities, due dates, and post comments without leaving the dashboard
📬
Customer Issue Triage System
Internal Operations Tool · AI-Powered
An AI-powered dashboard that reads Bond's customer success inbox, classifies reporting issues automatically using Claude, and surfaces them as a prioritized queue — with temperature scoring, SLA tracking, and direct JIRA ticket creation.
Every customer email triaged within minutes — no issues fall through the cracks
24-hour response SLA now enforced and visible across the team
Temperature scoring flags high-risk issues (finance escalations, exec involvement) automatically
Daily Slack digest keeps CS and data teams aligned without a meeting

The Foundation
What's Under the Hood
The infrastructure that makes every report possible — and that scales as Bond adds customers.
Snowflake + dbt Data Warehouse
Bond's Snowflake warehouse is the single source of truth for every financial number the company produces. Raw Postgres data flows through 287 SQL models before reaching any report — transforming payment events, registration records, and billing data into clean, auditable financial outputs.

The model covers everything from AR aging line items to accrual schedules, from Stripe reconciliation to space utilization. It's also the foundation for Bond's data-sharing partnerships.
